Bhuvan · ISRO overlays

      
Official 1:50,000 thematic maps served live by NRSC as WMS. View-only overlays — they render on the map but are not Earth Engine rasters, so zonal analysis and PDF reports do not apply to them.

      
      
      
      
      
      
      

        
        
      

      
NRSC servers can be slow at first load. If tiles stay blank, the state code may differ on Bhuvan or the service is briefly down.

      
      
      
      
    


    

      
Bhuvan APIs (daily token)

      
Paste your access token from bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/api (expires daily). Pick an API; {lat}/{lon} auto-fill from the Site-Brief point or map centre, {lat1}…{lon2} from the two route taps. Paths follow the portal docs — edit them if your doc page shows a different path.

Air Dispersion Model

      
AERMOD-formulation dispersion computing what arrives at every facade of a building, from multiple point, line and area sources.

      

        This is not EPA AERMOD. It implements the published AERMOD dispersion equations, but without AERMET, AERMAP terrain, PRIME building downwash or EPA validation. Not valid for compliance. Full detail in the model panel.
      


      
      
Pick a point — the footprint comes from OpenStreetMap, or a box is used.

      

        

        

      

      

      
Scene & model inputs

      
Reads the land cover, built-up surface and volume, road network and terrain within a radius, builds a 3D scene, and derives the dispersion inputs from that data instead of asking you to guess them.

Sunlight & solar

Sun path on the map

      
The 3D sun dome, projected onto the ground at your point — so azimuths line up with real compass directions on the map.

      
      
      

        
        
        
      

      

        
        
        
        
      

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      


      
Building shadows

      
Casts the real shadow of every mapped building around your point, using its actual height, for the date & time above.

      
      
      
      
Rooftop solar potential

      
Roof segments with orientation, pitch, area and modelled annual irradiance from the Google Solar API. India is covered at BASE quality (0.25 m/pixel).

      

      
      
      
      

      
All buildings in a region

      
Analyses every rooftop inside your Region of Interest. The Solar API has no bulk endpoint, so this costs one billable call per building — you will see the count and confirm before anything is spent.
